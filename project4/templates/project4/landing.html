About the study

We are comparing two interfaces that a movie recommender can use to learn the taste of a new user. You will use both of them, one after the other, and answer three short questions after each one. The session takes about 15 minutes.

We record which movies you saw, the order you gave them, and how long each task took. No personal data beyond your participant code, age group and movie-watching frequency is stored, and you can stop at any time by closing the page.

Dataset and preference model

Movies come from the IMDB 5000 Movie Dataset. Each movie is described by a feature vector x and the utility of a user is modelled as U(x) = wᵀx. Rankings are modelled with a Plackett-Luce extension of Bradley-Terry, which reduces to the pairwise model when only two movies are compared.
